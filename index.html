Ottershire – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Ottershire is a cozy village-building game for Android published by SvenSite ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

1. Short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google processes some data to show and measure ads (see section 3).
  • We do not sell personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress (buildings, resources, settings, achievements) in the app's private storage on your device. This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The "Back-up and restore" feature creates a text code on your device that you can copy yourself; we never see it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ottershire uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads that players can choose to watch, for example to speed up a build. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads. This is used to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve their services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the in-game menu under "Ad privacy" (shown where required).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device to tell you when a build is finished. No information is sent anywhere for this.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – used by the ad SDK to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID – used by the ad SDK as described above.
  • Notifications – used for the local "build finished" notification (optional).

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties, except for the ad provider described in section 3, which receives data when ads are requested or shown.

7. Children

Ottershire is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you control it: delete the app to remove it. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the options in section 3 and Google's own privacy tools. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example access, deletion or objection); you can contact us with any question.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/svenboyy/ottershire-privacy/issues.
